### Short overview of the idea:
When you're trying to become a different person, you build habits. But when the person you want to be is too different 
from who you are rn, you end up building too many habits.
It's difficult to remember them all. That's why ~~you need~~ I need a reminder and a bit of help in managing all of that.
Thus, in this app you will be able to track your habits/rules, build new ones and just kinda remember who you are.

### Ideas:
* enum for habits: **implemented, in progress, for later**.
* timer + task
* random habit of the day to remember
* a personality page with options for things you like, your inspirations
